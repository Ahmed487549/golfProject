ABOUT US

Home to a 46-bay, multi-tier, floodlit driving range, powered by Toptracer Range technology. Complemented by a practice green and bunker, coffee shop and American Golf Store.

There truly is something for everyone as we also boast two outdoor 18-hole dinosaur-themed crazy golf courses.

TOPTRACER RANGE

Looking for somewhere to enjoy quality time with friends and family or simply have some "me" time? Our floodlit 46-bay multi-tier range features market-leading Toptracer technology that everyone can enjoy.

Open until late all week, nothing quite beats a chilled evening session on the range with food and drinks delivered straight to your bay.

ADVENTURE GOLF

Adventure awaits you!

With two epic 18-hole outdoor crazy golf courses plus a café serving delicious treats, whether you're a big kid or a little kid, there really is something for everyone.

The perfect place to enjoy some quality time with the family and get some fresh air!

GOLF LESSONS

Our pros are truly dedicated to your improvement and aim for every student to leave having learnt something and feeling more confident about their golf game.

Whether you are beginning your golfing journey or simply looking to improve your game, our PGA-qualified professionals offer tailored lessons to best fit your golfing needs.
